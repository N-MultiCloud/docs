Symptom-oriented troubleshooting recipes for OPNsense. Topics cover NAT
issues, log inspection, captive portal, MTU/PMTU, gateway monitoring, and
common boot/upgrade failure modes.

## Working Rules

- Read-only vendor source.
- Look here first when symptoms are clear but the root cause isn't — these
  recipes are vetted by the upstream community.
